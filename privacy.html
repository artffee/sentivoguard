Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary first, formal language second. SentivoGuard is built around a simple principle: scanning happens on your device, and the only data leaves it is described below, including account and license checks.

The 30-second version

  • We collect: your email (when you sign up) and your license JWT (which contains your email + plan).
  • We don't collect: file contents from scans, browsing history, telemetry about how you use the app, or any device fingerprints.
  • Disk scans run entirely on your machine. Findings stay on your machine unless you click "↗ VT" (sends only the file's SHA-256 hash to VirusTotal).
  • Nova AI sends only the conversation text you type to Anthropic. Never file contents, never data from other tools.
  • Cookies are minimal: sg_session (a random session token). It is HttpOnly + Secure.
  • Signing out revokes your session. It does not delete your server-side account; contact us for an account data request.

What we collect

From the marketing site

Vercel, our hosting provider, logs basic HTTP request metadata (IP, user-agent, timestamp, requested path). We don't run any analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts on the marketing pages.

When you sign up

We collect your email address and a hashed (scrypt) password. These are stored in a private Redis account record, along with email-verification status and billing references. The browser receives only a random session token. Password hashes never appear in cookies. The same account works across browsers. Verification and password-reset links expire after 30 minutes and can be used once.

When you buy a paid plan

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see your card details — we use signed Stripe webhooks and the subscription status to update the account associated with its Stripe customer ID. We store customer and subscription IDs, plan, payment coverage and billing-review status. Stripe's privacy policy applies to their handling of your payment data.

Inside the desktop app

File scans, quarantine, the encrypted vault, and the real-time watcher all run entirely on your computer. Findings never leave your machine unless you opt in:

  • Clicking ↗ VT on a finding sends the file's SHA-256 hash (no contents) to VirusTotal.
  • Sending a Nova AI message sends only your typed text to Anthropic's Claude API.
  • Activating Nova requires pasting your own Anthropic API key — we never see it.

Desktop activation and periodic renewal send the signed license, including email and subscription reference, to sentivoguard.com. Paid licenses expire within three days unless renewed. The Nova API key is stored using operating-system encryption in the desktop app. Nova sends your conversation history and a system prompt to Anthropic.

What we don't collect

  • File contents from disk scans
  • The list of files in your Downloads / Temp folders
  • Quarantined file metadata
  • Vault entries (everything is AES-256-GCM encrypted client-side; we couldn't read it if we wanted to)
  • Application usage telemetry
  • Browsing history, network connections, device IDs

Account storage

Redis stores account records, hashed session and action tokens, rate-limit counters, and billing reconciliation records. Session tokens expire after 30 days; action tokens expire after 30 minutes. Billing event identifiers are retained for 90 days to prevent duplicate processing.

Cookies

The website uses sg_session, an opaque random token with HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax. It contains no email or password hash. The local desktop server uses a separate sg_local session cookie to protect its loopback API.

No tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies.

Retention

  • Session cookies — kept until you sign out, manually clear cookies, or 30 days pass without renewal.
  • License JWTs — valid until their exp claim (at most three days from issue).
  • Stripe records — retained under Stripe's policies and applicable record-keeping requirements.
  • Email logs — retained under Resend's configured service policy.

Your rights

  • Delete your account — contact us to request deletion of your central account record. Signing out only revokes the session.
  • Get a copy of your data — contact us to request your account data. Never share session cookies or license keys.
  • Cancel a paid plan — Stripe customer portal handles this. Email us if you need help.
  • Erase a Stripe record — email us with your customer ID and we'll request deletion within Stripe's retention rules.

Children

SentivoGuard isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a minor has signed up, contact us and we'll remove the account.

Changes

We'll update the "Effective" date at the top when this policy changes. Substantive changes will be announced via email to active members.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or breach notifications: hello@sentivoguard.com

This policy is intentionally short and concrete. If something in the product makes the privacy claims here inaccurate, please email us — we'll fix the product, then update this policy.
