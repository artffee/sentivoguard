Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary first, formal language second. SentivoGuard is built around a simple principle: scanning happens on your device, and the only data that leaves it is what you explicitly send.

The 30-second version

  • We collect: your email (when you sign up) and your license JWT (which contains your email + plan).
  • We don't collect: file contents from scans, browsing history, telemetry about how you use the app, or any device fingerprints.
  • Disk scans run entirely on your machine. Findings stay on your machine unless you click "↗ VT" (sends only the file's SHA-256 hash to VirusTotal).
  • Nova AI sends only the conversation text you type to Anthropic. Never file contents, never data from other tools.
  • Cookies are minimal: sg_session (your account) and sg_license (your license JWT). Both HttpOnly + Secure.
  • You can delete your account any time by clicking Sign out + clearing browser cookies. Per-device accounts mean nothing is stored on our servers.

What we collect

From the marketing site

Vercel, our hosting provider, logs basic HTTP request metadata (IP, user-agent, timestamp, requested path). We don't run any analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts on the marketing pages.

When you sign up

We collect your email address and a hashed (scrypt) password. Both are stored only in a signed JWT cookie set on your browser — there is no central user database in the current build. Logging in from a different browser requires a fresh signup.

When you buy a paid plan

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see your card details — Stripe sends us a webhook saying "this email paid for the Plus tier", and we mint a license JWT for that email. Stripe's privacy policy applies to their handling of your payment data.

Inside the desktop app

File scans, quarantine, the encrypted vault, and the real-time watcher all run entirely on your computer. Findings never leave your machine unless you opt in:

  • Clicking ↗ VT on a finding sends the file's SHA-256 hash (no contents) to VirusTotal.
  • Sending a Nova AI message sends only your typed text to Anthropic's Claude API.
  • Activating Nova requires pasting your own Anthropic API key — we never see it.

What we don't collect

  • File contents from disk scans
  • The list of files in your Downloads / Temp folders
  • Quarantined file metadata
  • Vault entries (everything is AES-256-GCM encrypted client-side; we couldn't read it if we wanted to)
  • Application usage telemetry
  • Browsing history, network connections, device IDs

Third parties

Anthropic Conversation text you type into Nova Each Nova message (Plus only)

Cookies

We set two cookies, both HttpOnly + Secure + SameSite=Lax:

  • sg_session — your authenticated session. Contains email, scrypt password hash, and (optionally) your license JWT. 30-day expiry.
  • sg_license — alternative authentication via license key alone, without an account. 30-day expiry.

No tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies.

Retention

  • Session cookies — kept until you sign out, manually clear cookies, or 30 days pass without renewal.
  • License JWTs — valid until their exp claim (typically 1 year from issue).
  • Stripe records — retained per Stripe's policy, typically 7 years for tax purposes.
  • Email logs — retained by Resend for ~30 days for delivery debugging.

Your rights

  • Delete your account — sign out and clear your browser cookies. There's no central user record to delete in the current build.
  • Get a copy of your data — your sg_session cookie IS your data. Decode it at jwt.io to inspect.
  • Cancel a paid plan — Stripe customer portal handles this. Email us if you need help.
  • Erase a Stripe record — email us with your customer ID and we'll request deletion within Stripe's retention rules.

Children

SentivoGuard isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a minor has signed up, contact us and we'll remove the account.

Changes

We'll update the "Effective" date at the top when this policy changes. Substantive changes will be announced via email to active members.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or breach notifications: hello@sentivoguard.com

This policy is intentionally short and concrete. If something in the product makes the privacy claims here inaccurate, please email us — we'll fix the product, then update this policy.
