Know what a result means

These are heuristic checks, not a replacement for Windows Defender or a comprehensive antivirus engine. Binary checks use metadata; some folders and file content are skipped. A result with no findings does not guarantee safety.

VPN, encrypted messaging, P2P transfer and hotspot sharing are in development and provide no protection in this release.
